This abundance of data has mostly been utilized for retrospective reporting rather than predictive planning, despite more than thirty years of data collection. A regionally adapted counterpart would be extremely beneficial for South Africa, which has one of the worst crime rates in the world and a very overworked police force. Using publicly accessible data and cutting-edge machine learning algorithms, CrimecastZA seeks to close this gap.         Requirements Business Problem 	 	 	 	 	[5] Provide a detailed description of the business problem if you have one that this project must address.
